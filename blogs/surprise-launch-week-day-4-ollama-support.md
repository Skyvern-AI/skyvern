**TL;DR**

One toggle → your automations run on-device, no cloud hop.



<pre><code class="language-jsx"># .env
ENABLE_OLLAMA=true
LLM_KEY=OLLAMA
OLLAMA_MODEL=qwen2.5:7b-instruct
OLLAMA_SERVER_URL=http://host.docker.internal:11434
</code></pre>



**Why you’ll care**

-   <strong>Zero data leaves the box</strong> – ideal for air-gapped or PII-heavy flows,
-   <strong>Drop-in swap</strong> – test the latest gemma model or qwen model with a one line change
-   <strong>Run custom fine-tunes -</strong> fine-tune your own open source LLM and try it out with Skyvern
