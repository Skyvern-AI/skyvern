<h3 id="drumroll-please%E2%80%A6">Drumroll please…</h3>



Exciting news: our integration with Zapier is now live! Zapier is an automation platform tailored to freelancers, small businesses, and non-technical users. We're excited to be one of the 5,000+ supported integrators available, extending your Zaps into the browser.



<h3 id="why-integrate-into-zapier">Why Integrate into Zapier?</h3>



A workflow builder inside a workflow builder? You might think we’re crazy (and maybe we are), but when you really think about it, we serve different purposes. Skyvern allows you to reach into the browser and access sites without APIs, extending the possibilities of your Zaps. Want to onboard a new customer and automate government form submission? Need to make a system for managing inventory that automates invoice downloads? Getting ready to start a new outbound campaign with contact us forms? You can do all of this and more with a Skyvern integration ⚡

Here are a few ideas to get you cookin’

-   Real estate: auto-submit “Contact Us” forms to get in touch with potential buyers, sellers, and renters
-   Recruiting: Automate job applications and sync data between platforms
-   Legal: Auto-fill case evaluation forms and other required paperwork
-   E-commerce: Manage inventory by auto-downloading invoices from online stores
-   Government: Auto-fill forms and gather public legal info
-   Sales: Scrape leads from competitor sites and other forms of competitor analysis

Happy Automating!
