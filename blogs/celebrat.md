🌟 TWELVE THOUSAND 🌟

Here are some interesting star milestone facts

1.  Going from 0 - 5K took as long as going from 5K - 12K
2.  We stopped looking at star count as a top line metric when we launched Skyvern Cloud
3.  Star count is not correlated with our discord user growth
4.  Companies stopped caring about our star count after 5K Github stars -- 5K hit the sweet spot of credibility
5.  Star count is not the same as product market fit
6.  It is a great proxy for market pull + interest
7.  I still remember the name of each of our first 60 stars
8.  The biggest source of Stars for us has been AI newsletters featuring us after our launch
9.  Github gives surprisingly good stats on the source of traffic for your Github repo
10.  We've (successfully) resisted the urge to spam our Github star friends, despite tons of inbound suggesting it's easy to do so
