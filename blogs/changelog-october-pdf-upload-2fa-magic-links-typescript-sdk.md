Hello everyone,

With halloween behind us, I bring a lot of exciting Skyvern product improvement. Get ready, we shipped a lot of cool features last month!



<h1 id="pdf-sop-uploadupload-a-pdf-describing-your-workflow-and-let-skyvern-build-it">PDF / SOP Upload - Upload a PDF describing your workflow, and let Skyvern build it!</h1>



Were you tired of building workflows by hand? Did you already have a ton of SOPs in place and you just want to automate them all?

Well.. wait no more! <a href="https://app.skyvern.com/workflows" rel="noreferrer">Go to our workflows page</a>, click the import button, and let Skyvern do its magic

<h1 id="2fa-upgradesauthenticate-with-magic-links">2FA Upgrades - authenticate with magic links</h1>



Tired of websites that send you a magic link to authenticate? Find it hard to do those automations?

Well.. we just expanded our 2FA support to handle these cases! Just forward the emails to Skyvern's endpoints, and Skyvern will log in and automate for you!



<p>Want to learn more? Just read the docs here: <a href="https://www.skyvern.com/docs/credentials/totp/">https://www.skyvern.com/docs/credentials/totp/</a></p>

<h1 id="typescript-sdk">Typescript SDK</h1>



Skyvern just got a brand new SDK for all of our Typescript / Javascript friends out there. Don't waste your time doing custom http requests - `npm install @skyvern/client` instead!



<p><a href="https://www.npmjs.com/package/@skyvern/client">https://www.npmjs.com/package/@skyvern/client</a></p>
