Founders are building job application SaaS platforms on Skyvern — without writing a single scraper or bot. Here’s how it works 👇

Most job boards are painful to automate:<br>❌ No public APIs<br>❌ Different layouts for every site<br>❌ 2FA, CAPTCHA, file uploads

But this is exactly where most hiring happens.

Instead of fighting manual processes, teams are now launching browser-native job automation services — powered quietly by Skyvern in the background.

Skyvern isn’t a job bot.<br>It’s browser automation infrastructure.

Not a scraper.

Not a macro recorder.

Not site-specific scripts.

It’s an AI-native agent layer that sees, clicks, types, and submits — like a human — across any browser interface.

Use cases founders are building today:<br>✅ Job seekers auto-applying to hundreds of roles per week<br>✅ Recruiters automating submissions across niche job boards<br>✅ SaaS founders offering "done-for-you" resume and application services<br>✅ Career services teams scaling client deliverables without hiring extra staff

One founder is processing 1,000+ job applications per day with a Skyvern-powered platform.
