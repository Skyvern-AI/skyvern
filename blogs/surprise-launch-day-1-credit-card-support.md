Today we’re launching a **major super-power** for your browser automations: **securely add credit cards once and let Skyvern handle checkouts end-to-end**

* * *



<h3 id="why-this-matters"><strong>Why this matters</strong></h3>



-   <strong>One-click setup</strong> – Save a card in seconds; [via API](https://docs.skyvern.com/api-reference/api-reference/credentials/create-credential) or via our UI. Skyvern stores it inside a secure vault.
-   <strong>Hands-free automations</strong> – Your agents can now pay for subscriptions, place test orders, validate carts, or top-up balances while you sleep.
-   <strong>Enterprise-grade security</strong> – Card numbers are never exposed to your workflows; only short-lived network tokens reach the page.

* * *



<h3 id="get-started"><strong>Get started</strong></h3>



**1.Add card →** Navigate to [Credentials](https://app.skyvern.com/credentials) and click **“Credit Card”**

**2.Use in any workflow block →** Reference {{payment\_method:"My Amex"}} in checkout steps—Skyvern auto-fills and submits the form, then redacts the page snapshot.

3.Let your orders process autonomously!
