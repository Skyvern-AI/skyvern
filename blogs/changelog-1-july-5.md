<h2 id="1detailed-billing-ie-how-much-did-i-spend-on-that-task">#1 - Detailed billing (ie how much did I spend on that task?!)</h2>



You asked, we delivered🎉 You can now see how much your tasks cost you in Skyvern's task billing page 😹

I bet you're wondering.. why did we launch Skyvern without this feature? Well... we wouldn't need to build this if no one signed up to use Skyvern!

One of the most frequent messages we got were questions about how they can cancel / rollback their running tasks.

Worry not! Skyvern now supports that feature 😄
