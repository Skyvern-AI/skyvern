Posting on socials / blogging feels really unnatural for me. I've always been the kind of person that focuses on their own thing, keeps quiet and moves along in life<br><br>But.. after reading a lot about how marketing / sales works.. I decided to give this a try<br><br>"I signed up for your platform in September but we didn't need it until now.. but I kept reading your weekly blog posts!"<br><br>I heard this in a customer call the other day and it floored me. I had read theories about why posting regularly was important.. but... <br><br>You know the addage "Sales is all about timing?" Your goal is to find people who are having the problem right now so you can solve it for them<br>So.. what do you do with everyone who isn't having the problem?<br><br>It turns out that posting regularly can help you stay top of mind with your prospects so they think of you when they are actively looking for a solution to their problem<br><br>So to everyone else out there who isn't sure about it.. just bite the bullet and try it for a month and see how it goes!<br><br>I would never have heard the positive feedback had I not given it a shot
