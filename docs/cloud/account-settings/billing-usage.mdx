Skyvern bills per **credit**: each browser action the AI takes during a run consumes credits. You pay for real work, not for agents created or credentials stored.

## Plans

Go to **Settings → Billing** to see available plans and your credit usage. To get there, click your organization name at the top of the sidebar, choose **Organization settings**, then click **Billing**. On the Free and Hobby plans, the **Upgrade plan** link at the bottom of the sidebar opens the same page.

**Free** is available to all new accounts with a one-time credit grant and zero commitment. **Hobby** and **Pro** are monthly subscriptions. Click **Upgrade** on the Hobby or Pro card to subscribe. Once you're subscribed, the page shows your current plan and an option to upgrade further.

## How credits are consumed

Skyvern charges per step the AI attempts: **50 credits ($0.05) per step**. In each step the AI looks at the page and takes one or more actions, such as clicking, typing, selecting or extracting. A short login is usually a few steps; a long multi-page run takes more. Failed and retried steps count, because each one is attempted.

To see how your credits are being used, open **Settings → Billing** and expand **Usage Breakdown** under **Credit Usage**.

<Tip>
Reduce credit usage by being specific in your prompts. "Find information about pricing" causes the AI to explore multiple pages. "Extract the monthly price from the pricing table on /pricing" gets there directly.
</Tip>

## Credit usage

The **Credit Usage** section shows how many credits you've used out of your allowance, the percentage used, and how many remain. On the Free plan, credits are a one-time grant and don't reset monthly. Credits are deducted as runs execute in real time. If you run out, new runs won't start until you add credits or upgrade your plan.

## FAQ

<Accordion title="How do credits work?">
Each step the AI attempts (clicking, typing, navigating, extracting) costs 50 credits ($0.05), as shown at the top of Settings → Billing. Credits are deducted in real time as runs execute. Free accounts receive a one-time credit grant. Paid plans include a monthly credit allowance that resets each billing cycle. Unused included credits don't roll over.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I buy more credits?">
Yes. If you exhaust your included allowance, you can purchase additional credits from the Billing page. You can also upgrade to a higher plan for a larger monthly allowance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
Yes. Downgrade or cancel your subscription from the Billing page. The change takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle, and you keep access to your credits until then.
</Accordion>
