It's funny how Sales, Recruiting, and Fundraising follow the exact same process



<h3 id="step-1-set-up-a-google-sheet">Step 1. Set up a google sheet</h3>





<h3 id="step-2-throw-in-some-leads">Step 2. Throw in some leads</h3>



-   Sales? Find promising companies
-   Recruiting? Find promising candidates
-   Fundraising? Find promising investors



<h3 id="step-3-reach-out-to-them">Step 3. Reach out to them</h3>



-   Sales? People you know > Warm Intros > Cold outbound
-   Recruiting? People you know > Warm Intros > Cold outbound
-   Fundraising? Investors you know > Warn Intros > Cold outbound



<h3 id="step-4-run-a-parallel-process">Step 4. Run a parallel process</h3>



-   Sales? Track pipeline $$
-   Recruiting? Track pipeline candidates
-   Fundraising? Track pipeline VCs



<h3 id="step-5-follow-up-relentlessly">Step 5. Follow up relentlessly</h3>



-   Sales? Customer wants to do a follow up? Book on Monday
-   Recruiting? Candidate not ready til next month? Reach out
-   Fundraising? Investor will get back to you? Assume it's a no and move on
