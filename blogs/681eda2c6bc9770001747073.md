Skyvern is browser-native automation powered by AI.

It mimics human interaction — clicks, types, navigates, uploads — and works across any web interface.

If it happens in a browser, Skyvern can automate it.

Here are 6 more powerful, production-ready use cases:

* * *

🔹 𝟭. 𝗚𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗿𝗻𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗣𝗼𝗿𝘁𝗮𝗹𝘀 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗹𝗶𝗮𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗦𝘂𝗯𝗺𝗶𝘀𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀<br>Teams use Skyvern to log into state and federal portals to submit reports, upload forms, and confirm receipt — all without custom code or integrations.<br>→ Works with multi-step flows, 2FA, and document uploads.

* * *

🔹 𝟮. 𝗩𝗲𝗻𝗱𝗼𝗿 𝗣𝗼𝗿𝘁𝗮𝗹𝘀 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗜𝗻𝘃𝗼𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝗥𝗲𝘁𝗿𝗶𝗲𝘃𝗮𝗹<br>Instead of logging into 15–30 different vendor sites each month to download statements, finance teams use Skyvern agents to do it all — automatically.<br>→ Download, rename, organize, and store invoices in one flow.

* * *

🔹 𝟯. 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗰𝘂𝗿𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗶𝗻 𝗟𝗲𝗴𝗮𝗰𝘆 𝗘𝗥𝗣𝘀<br>Skyvern runs procurement workflows inside legacy web-based ERP tools.<br>→ Submit purchase orders, extract pricing, and sync data across systems — without needing an API.

* * *

🔹 𝟰. 𝗜𝗻𝘀𝘂𝗿𝗮𝗻𝗰𝗲 𝗤𝘂𝗼𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 + 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗶𝘀𝗼𝗻 𝗙𝗹𝗼𝘄𝘀<br>Agents and platforms use Skyvern to submit identical quote requests across multiple provider portals.<br>→ Uploads documents, fills multi-page forms, and tracks pricing responses in a central database.

* * *

🔹 𝟱. 𝗛𝗲𝗮𝗹𝘁𝗵𝗰𝗮𝗿𝗲 𝗗𝗮𝘁𝗮 𝗦𝘆𝗻𝗰 (𝗘𝗛𝗥 → 𝗕𝗶𝗹𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴/𝗦𝗰𝗵𝗲𝗱𝘂𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴)<br>Skyvern securely transfers structured patient data from EHRs into scheduling and billing systems — preserving integrity, reducing friction, and saving hours of manual work.<br>→ Handles HIPAA-sensitive flows with credential injection and audit logging.

* * *

𝟲. 𝗥𝗲𝗮𝗹 𝗘𝘀𝘁𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝗟𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗗𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗶𝗯𝘂𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻<br>Teams post listings to Zillow, Apartments.com, and 10+ platforms through Skyvern — complete with images, pricing, and formatting requirements.<br>→ Create once, publish everywhere — no duplicate entry needed.

* * *

These are real browser workflows being automated every day.

→ No scraping<br>→ No API dependencies<br>→ No custom dev work<br>→ Just a smart, scalable agent in the browser
