<h3 id="hear-ye-hear-ye">Hear Ye, Hear Ye!</h3>



We are thrilled to announce our integration in Pabbly Connect! Pabbly is a leading SaaS application suite that specializes in automations. With around 18.5k active users and 44k subscribers on YouTube, we are excited to embed Skyvern inside this cutting-edge automation tool.



<h3 id="why-integrate-into-pabbly">Why Integrate into Pabbly?</h3>



Integrations are common ways to feed your SaaS tools into your automations. Pulling meeting notes with one integrator, parsing them with another, completing data entry with a third — the power in this sequence is pulling data from your existing tools and feeding them into your automations. But this sequence is often left with gaps. Most integrations are locked into vendors with APIs and unable to reach into the browser, so seeing this window of opportunity, we partnered with Pabbly!

The value of this integration was clear: bringing Pabbly into the browser. Running Skyvern Agent from Pabbly’s UI allows businesses to take their automations to a new level. Task automations in a CRM? Fill out random Contact Us forms? Automating file downloads? Government form submissions? All powered by your own Google Sheets? These are all now possible with this integration 🙂

Here are some use cases that might inspire your Skyvern integrations in Pabbly!

-   E-commerce: invoice extraction from online retailers, automated procurement + purchasing, competitor price scraping
-   Government: automated form filings, scraping public legal data
-   Sales: “Contact Us” form submissions, automated bookings, lead scraping in competitive marketplaces
-   Recruiting: automated job applications, transferring data between systems
-   Accounting: automated invoice downloading

Happy Automating!
