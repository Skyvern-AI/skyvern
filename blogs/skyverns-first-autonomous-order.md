Hit a major Skyvern milestone

Skyvern finished its first autonomous order. End to End.

This wasn't a demonstration -- this was a real order being placed to automate something very core to a business we've been working closely with

Here are all of the steps it has to perform for it to work correctly:

1.  Navigate to an e-commerce website
2.  Log in
3.  Clear the cart if there's already products there
4.  Go through a list of products
5.  Check if the specified variant exists and is available for purchase
6.  Confirm the price of the product is correct
7.  Add it to cart.. in the specified quantity
8.  Go to the cart, and validate that the cart price + shipping is less than the expected amount
9.  Validate that the cart has the correct products + variants
10.  Update the shipping address information to the correct recipient
11.  Fill in the credit card information + address
12.  Do 2FA verification on the credit card website
13.  Finish the transaction
14.  Extract the confirmation number

I never thought we would see AI Agents complete such complex workflows -- but we're finally breaking the glass and adding real value.

PS: The best part? The entire order was completed in Spanish
