<h3 id="new-integration-alert">New Integration Alert!</h3>



Skyvern is now officially an integration in viaSocket! viaSocket is an AI-driven workflow automation platform that makes it easy to connect apps and automate tasks—without writing a single line of code. With simple triggers and workflows, teams can build powerful automations that bridge the gaps between their favorite web applications.



<h3 id="why-integrate-into-viasocket">Why Integrate into viaSocket?</h3>



viaSocket users want speed, flexibility, and automation that works where APIs fall short. That’s exactly where Skyvern comes in.

Skyvern allows you to interact with any website—even those without APIs—by submitting forms, scraping data, clicking buttons, and more, all from natural-language prompts. Paired with viaSocket, you can create workflows that go beyond what APIs alone can deliver.

Here’s how users are already combining the two:

-   <strong>Automate behind logins</strong>: Pull analytics from your SaaS dashboards, manage records from your education or HR portal, or auto-complete CRM actions after every sales call.
-   <strong>Scale repetitive actions across sites</strong>: Submit to directories, fill out lead-gen forms, mass-apply to jobs, or handle bulk e-commerce purchases—at scale.
-   <strong>Streamline high-friction workflows</strong>: Tackle government forms, insurance quotes, or profile configurations—no matter how many fields or steps are involved.

Our block is now live in viaSocket—just drag, connect, and describe what you want Skyvern to do in the browser.

✨ Happy Automating!
