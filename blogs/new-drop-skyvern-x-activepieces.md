<h3 id="trumpets-please">Trumpets, please!</h3>



We’re excited to share that Skyvern is now officially an integration in Activepieces!

Activepieces is an open-source business automation platform that lets teams build powerful workflows using over 300 integrations.



<h3 id="why-integrate-into-activepieces">Why Integrate into Activepieces?</h3>



Activepieces users love flexibility and power—and they’ve been finding ways to combine it with Skyvern to unlock new kinds of browser-based automation. So we decided to make it official.

Skyvern allows you to interact with websites that don’t have APIs—think submitting forms, scraping data, clicking buttons, and more—all from natural-language prompts. Activepieces brings end-to-end orchestration. Together, they help teams build powerful workflows that go beyond what APIs can offer alone.

Here’s how users are already combining the two:

-   <strong>Automate behind logins</strong>: Pull reports from your logistics platform, manage student data from your education portal, or auto-complete tedious CRM tasks after every call.
-   <strong>Scale repetitive actions across sites</strong>: Submit to SEO directories, fill out lead-gen contact forms, mass-apply to jobs, or purchase on key platforms—at scale.
-   <strong>Handle tedious, high-friction workflows</strong>: Complete government forms, generate insurance quotes, or configure profiles—no matter how many steps or fields.

Our block is live in Activepieces, go check it out! Just drag, connect, and describe what you want to do in the browser.

Happy Automating!
