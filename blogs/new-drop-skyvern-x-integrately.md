<h3 id="by-decree-of-the-crown">By decree of the crown!</h3>



I am thrilled to announce our Skyvern integration in Integrately! As implied in their name, Integrately is all about unifying your tech stack. They’re also all about meeting their users where they’re at, making a one-click automation tool that is accessible to people of all skill levels.



<h3 id="why-integrate-into-integrately">Why Integrate into Integrately?</h3>



Integrately is well-connected, having integrations for 1,200+ apps. Since Integrately caters to a wide direct and enterprise audience, with a robust integration library to match, Skyvern is well positioned to extend their users’ automations. Are you someone looking to automate your job’s form filling, pulling from Airtable and automating data entry in a portal? Are you an enterprise looking to streamline outbound, getting data from HubSpot and automating contact form submission directly on their site? Since Skyvern can uniquely automate on sites that don’t have an API, we bring their automations one step further and into the browser.

Here are a few use cases that might catch your eye

-   Taxes and Accounting: Automatically download e-receipts or invoices
-   Healthcare: Automate appointment bookings and data transfer
-   Recruiting: Automate job applications and sync data between platforms
-   Sales: automate data transfer when switching from one outbound tool to another and scrape leads for a competitor analysis
-   Legal and Government: Auto-fill required forms and gather public legal info

Happy Automating!
