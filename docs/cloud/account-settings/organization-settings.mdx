Everything in Skyvern (agents, credentials, runs, API keys) belongs to an **organization**. One is created automatically when you sign up.

## Organization details

Open **Settings** in the sidebar. The Settings page shows your organization name and current plan. The organization name appears throughout the UI and in API responses.

To rename your organization, update the **Organization Name** field and click **Save**.

## Team members

Plans that support multiple users (Pro and Enterprise) show a **Members** section below the organization details.

### Inviting members

Click **Invite Member**, enter their email address, and send the invitation. They'll receive an email with a link to join your organization. Once they accept, they can use the organization's shared agents and runs according to their assigned role.

### Removing members

Click the **remove icon** next to a member's name to revoke their access. They lose access to all organization resources immediately.

<Warning>
When removing a team member, also review your [API keys](/cloud/account-settings/api-keys). Organization API keys have administrator-level access and remain active until they are rotated.
</Warning>

<Note>
Role-based access control is available on **Enterprise** plans. Organization administrators can manage organization settings, credentials and secrets, API keys, and billing. Non-admin members cannot call those sensitive organization APIs.
</Note>
