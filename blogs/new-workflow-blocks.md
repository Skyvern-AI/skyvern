Hey Everyone! Welcome to Skyvern's inaugral launch week. We're ending the year with a bang: 5 Updates. 5 Days.

New Workflow blocks spotted!<br><br>We just released 5 new workflow blocks that are going to change the game<br><br>Our old blocks were super confusing, and tried to do too many things at once. We re-structured them to follow a much simpler pattern:<br><br>1. Navigation Block<br>This block is quite simple. Give Skyvern a "Goal", and Skyvern will navigate until it has accomplished its goal! It's much more streamlined and takes all of the "power" of the previous task block, with none of the confusion<br><br>2. Action Block<br>Don't want Skyvern to accept a goal? Use Action block to give it a single action to take on a page. Click a button. Fill in some text. Select an option. Upload a file. It's perfect for when you need a little bit of granular control<br><br>3. Extraction block<br>Want to extract some data? This is the perfect block for you. It will not do any navigation.. instead it will turn the page contents into structured text for you<br><br>4. Validation block<br>Need to validate the state of the form is correct before submitting? Want to assert that you have the correct number of items added to cart? Want to bail if there are no invoices found? We have the perfect purpose built block for you :) <br><br>5. Wait block<br>Want Skyvern to chill while a website finishes processing a CSV? You can do that now with the Wait block!!
