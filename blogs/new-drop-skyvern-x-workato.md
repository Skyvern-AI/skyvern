<h3 id="let-it-be-known-to-those-far-and-wide">Let it be known to those far and wide!</h3>



Our latest integration in Workato is here! Workato is an enterprise automation platform that helps businesses integrate apps and automate workflows at scale. It serves mid-to-large enterprises, empowering both IT teams and business users to streamline operations.



<h3 id="why-integrate-into-workato">Why Integrate into Workato?</h3>



In short: enterprises are our bread and butter, and Workato connects and automates within enterprises tech stacks. So, why do we focus on B2B services at Skyvern? Many businesses need to connect their sites to others to deliver on what they promise, but that can be tough when a site doesn’t offer an API. Dev teams spend tons of time trying to work around this with RPAs, and that’s where Skyvern comes in. Instead of getting stuck on API limits, Skyvern navigates right in the browser, avoiding the brittle scripts and getting past blockers with ease.

Here are some common B2B use cases for your Skyvern integration in Workato:

-   A variety of industries value <strong>invoice downloading</strong>, including **retail** for tracking online transactions, **supply chain logistics** for transport and vendor shipments, and **healthcare** to keep track of medical supplies
-   Auto-filling <strong>contact forms for lead generation</strong> has shown to be valuable for **real estate companies** looking to connect with potential buyers using property inquiry forms, or **legal services** looking to automate submissions on legal directories to get the word out
-   Automating <strong>job applications</strong> is a big one for the direct consumer, but for industries, **recruiting agencies** and **auto-apply B2C companies** value this service the most
-   <strong>Procurement</strong> is also a big, repetitive task often endured by **e-commerce companies** with order-and-ship services, **construction companies** that have endless bulk orders to process, and **hospitality services** looking to streamline food and beverage orders

Happy Automating!
