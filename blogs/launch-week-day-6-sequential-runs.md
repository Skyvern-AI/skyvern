Skyvern’s new **Sequential Runs** feature makes sure your workflows play nicely together — no more parallel runs stepping on each other’s toes or logging you out unexpectedly.

This builds on our mission to make automated browsing **reliable at scale**, especially when authentication or session-sensitive sites are involved.

Here’s what it unlocks:

-   <strong>Session-safe execution —</strong> runs now queue up and execute one after the other, so you never lose your login mid-workflow.
-   <strong>Predictable automation —</strong> no more surprises when two runs try to authenticate at the same time.
-   <strong>Workflow stability —</strong> your scripts finish cleanly without interference, keeping results consistent.

* * *



<h2 id="how-it-works"><strong>How it works</strong></h2>



1.  <strong>Enable Sequential Runs</strong> in your workflow settings.
2.  <strong>Launch workflows as usual</strong> — Skyvern automatically ensures only one run is active per session.
3.  <strong>Relax</strong> knowing your runs won’t fight for login state anymore.
