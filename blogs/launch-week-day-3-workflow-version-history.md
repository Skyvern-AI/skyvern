<h2 id="never-lose-your-favourite-workflow-setup-again"><strong>Never lose your favourite workflow setup again!</strong></h2>



When you’re building workflows, it’s easy to accidentally overwrite a prompt that was working beautifully—or remove a configuration you loved while experimenting with new ones. Until now, once those changes were made, they were gone for good.

With **Version History**, you can now browse through past versions of any workflow and restore the exact setup you want. This makes iterating safer, faster, and way less stressful.

* * *



<h2 id="how-it-works"><strong>How it works</strong></h2>



-   <strong>Build your workflow as usual</strong> — every change is automatically tracked in the background.
-   <strong>Open version history</strong> — see a list of all previous versions of your workflow, including prompts and configurations.
-   <strong>Restore instantly</strong> — if something breaks or you realize the old way worked better, just roll back to that version with a click.
-   <strong>Experiment with confidence</strong> — try new prompts, tweak logic, or adjust settings knowing you can always get back to a working setup.

* * *

We’re hoping this makes your Skyvern workflow building more forgiving and more fun. Iteration should feel safe, not nerve-wracking. Version History is a step toward making that possible.
