Being in the job market is tough. It’s a grueling process of continuously filling out applications, hours of time spent filling out the same questions again and again for jobs that often don’t give the applicant the courtesy of a rejection email. It fits all of the criteria for torture.

Skyvern has been helping people silently automate this for the past few months… and we thought we would make it easier for everyone!

🧠 How does it work?

1.  Bulk submit: include the links for hundreds of different jobs and send it off
2.  No time spent customizing: the AI automatically tailors responses to more complex questions using information your provide + information from your resume
3.  Set it and forget it: run hundreds concurrently and receive feedback from the job poster directly in your inbox

🚀 How can you get started?

1.  Go to [https://app.skyvern.com/jobs](https://app.skyvern.com/jobs)
2.  Upload your resume
3.  Paste a list of jobs
4.  Fill in a little bit of extra information that might be valuable
5.  Click run and watch it apply to jobs automatically 🙂
