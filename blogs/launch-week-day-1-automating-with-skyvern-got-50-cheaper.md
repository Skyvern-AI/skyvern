<h3 id="ask-and-you-shall-receive-%F0%9F%A4%91">Ask and you shall receive 🤑</h3>



We heard you: Skyvern was too pricey! So we went back to the drawing board and found a way to bring costs down.

🚀 From now on, **Skyvern steps are 50% cheaper!**



<h3 id="what-changed">What changed?</h3>



**10¢ → 5¢ per step**

A step = anytime Skyvern visits or scans a page

That’s the heartbeat of your automation—every scan, every map, every action. And now, it costs half as much. Which means your automation dollar goes twice as far.



<h3 id="why-we%E2%80%99re-doing-this">Why we’re doing this</h3>



We’re starting off launch week with a bang!

We want you to go wild:

-   scrape bigger lists
-   automate longer forms
-   prototype faster
-   experiment and test freely,
-   and unlock ideas you didn’t think were feasible.

We want you to push your business, further your career, and get excited about automating without sweating the bill!
